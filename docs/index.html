One screen, every option

Everything a quick GIF needs, nothing it doesn't.

Any source

A video to trim, or a set of images to sequence and reorder. gifly detects which and adapts the timing controls.

Three formats

GIF (256 colors, selectable dither), WebP (smaller, lossy, quality slider), or APNG (lossless).

Crop & aspect

Free, 1:1, 16:9 or 9:16 - centered crop then scale to the width you pick, with the output height shown live.

Playback

Loop forever, once or N times; playback speed 0.25-4x; reverse and boomerang.

Fits your target

Discord / Slack / Twitter presets, or a target size in MB that gifly shrinks to - with a live size estimate before you export.

Save & share

Choose the folder and name, decide what happens if it exists, and copy the finished file to the clipboard to paste into a chat.
